About Me

Piumantha U. Kumara

Designer by trade, editor by instinct.

"Hi, I'm Piyumantha Udhaya Kumara, a passionate Graphic and Web Designer from Sri Lanka. I specialize in creating modern, visually appealing, and user-friendly designs that help businesses build a strong brand identity and online presence. I am dedicated to delivering high-quality work with creativity, attention to detail, and on-time delivery."

My work sits at the intersection of graphic design and motion — I don't just design a mark, I think about how it moves, how it sounds in a reel, and how it holds up across a feed, a storefront, and a screen. That dual lens, designer and editor, is what shapes every brief I take on.

Every project starts with the same question: what does this brand need to say, and to whom? From there I build systems, not just single assets — logo suites, color and type rules, layout grids, and motion templates that a business can keep using long after the first delivery.

Working with clients across different markets has taught me to balance a distinct creative point of view with what actually performs — designs that look premium and also convert. Attention to detail and hitting deadlines aren't an afterthought; they're the baseline everything else is built on.
